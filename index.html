About

I'm a postgraduate researcher at the University of Otago working on clinical natural language processing. My current focus is automated ICD coding: reading free-text clinical documents and predicting the diagnosis and procedure codes they support.

Most coding systems do well on common codes and struggle with rare ones. I'm interested in bringing external medical knowledge into models so that the long tail gets coded properly too.
